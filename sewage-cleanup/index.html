Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. When it happens in Surprise, Surprise Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Surprise

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
