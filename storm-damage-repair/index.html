When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Surprise

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

The repairs a storm leaves behind are tangled together, and sorting them out correctly calls for an experienced restoration crew. What seems like a small leak indoors can hide widespread water damage in wall cavities, roof decking and insulation, which turns into mold and structural decay unless it is identified and dried properly. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging and moisture meters to trace water paths and identify every affected area.

Tarping and boarding up only work when they are done with the right technique. Tarps put on the wrong way may funnel water into the building or tear loose in the wind. Our crews are experienced in storm damage mitigation and use the right materials and methods to give reliable temporary protection while permanent repairs are scheduled.

How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
